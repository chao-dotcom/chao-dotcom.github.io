The short version

I'm an Edge AI engineer. The interesting part of ML isn't the training run on a cluster — it's the moment you have to make that model run on the thing people actually hold: a camera, a headset, a battery-powered sensor that can't phone home. That's where I live: quantization, custom CUDA kernels, and squeezing latency until it fits the budget.

That thread runs through everything I build — a RAW→RGB camera ISP, a from-scratch object tracker, a face-enhancement pipeline on a Snapdragon NPU, an offline LLM assistant on a Raspberry Pi. Different industries, same moat: take the model off the cloud and make it real on hardware.

Right now I'm doing exactly that as an intern at Logitech, while wrapping up my M.S. in Computer Science at Rice (Dec 2026). When I'm not doing that, I'm grinding LeetCode (occasionally LeetCrying), or in the pool pretending I'm a seal discovering recursion. Somewhere on the dark side of the moon, a Bowie fan named Norman approves.
